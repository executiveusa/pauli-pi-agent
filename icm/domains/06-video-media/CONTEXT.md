# Video and Media

One job: analyze or produce media while keeping evidence coverage, storytelling, and production mechanics explicit.

## Owners

- `skills/video-watch/` — normalized evidence-aware video analysis and study mode.
- video/media packages and references registered in `skills/SKILLS_REGISTRY.md`.
- Mercury/voice components in agent/runtime packages.
- external Pauli montage/video projects are integrations or sibling products until code is explicitly brought into this repo.

## Rules

Declare what was actually reviewed: metadata, transcript, audio transcription, selected visuals, or full audiovisual review. Do not treat transcript-only understanding as visual evidence. Route narrative through Storytelling and course/knowledge use through Research + Memory.
