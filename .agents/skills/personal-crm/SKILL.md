# Personal CRM

## Record
People, organizations, context, relationship type, interactions, commitments, follow-ups, gratitude, and relevant projects.

## Rules
- Do not reduce people to lead scores by default.
- Preserve source/date for relationship facts.
- Separate private notes from public biography/collaborator copy.
- Never expose private contact details or correspondence on the public site without explicit approval.
- Flag stale/conflicting identity records for dedupe review.
- Create follow-up suggestions, not fabricated promises or messages sent without approval.

## Output
Private relationship records, reminders/suggestions, and public-projection candidates when specifically requested.
